Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878`.

`main.ts` writes one line — the most creative excuse a hardware wallet gives
for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM — to `/output/entry.txt`. The line
is validated before it is written: 1 to 200 characters, a single line, not
only white space, and no control characters.

The entry line:

> It is 3 AM, my firmware is asleep, and your thumbs are typing like they have had three coffees. Come back when the sun, and your judgement, are up.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
